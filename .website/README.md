## Do you know where nightmares come from?
They come on the nights the unicorns are not watching over you.

![Unicorn vs Nightmares gameplay](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Radrael/UnicornNight-js13k/refs/heads/main/media/gameplay-desktop.gif)

## Tonight, you are the one who watches. 
Place your unicorns across the dream and link them back to your castle. 

Collect the **stars** scattered along the way, and cover the monster's tiles to steal the ones it is sitting on. Spend the **powers on your cards**: draw more, play again, sweep an enemy off the board, or send in the little pink one that beats absolutely everything.

Reach the **monster's castle**, or **hold your stars through one whole turn** of its attacks, and the nightmare turns back into a dream. Then the next dream opens, a little darker than the last one.

How long can you keep the nightmares away?

## Controls

Use your `Mouse` on Desktop, `Touch` on Mobile.


Also playable on [Wavedash](https://wavedash.com/games/unicorn-vs-nightmares)

Have fun !
